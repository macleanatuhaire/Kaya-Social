Yes, you still need sunscreen

Melanin is a gift. It is not a force field.

There is a myth that dark skin does not need sunscreen. Here is the kind version of the truth.

Melanin does protect you. It gives dark skin some natural defence against the sun, roughly the equivalent of a low SPF. That is a head start, and not enough on its own.

The sun still leaves its mark. UV rays darken dark spots, uneven patches and acne scars, and keep them from fading. If you want an even, glowing complexion, sunscreen is part of the plan.

Skin cancer is rarer on dark skin, but it is not unheard of. When it does happen, it is often found late, and often in places we forget to protect, like the palms, the soles of the feet and under the nails. Know your skin, and see a doctor about any spot that changes.

“We live near the equator, so the sun is never really off.”

In Uganda the sun is strong all year, even when the sky is cloudy. Daily sunscreen is not a luxury, it is part of looking after the skin you are in.

Keep it easy

Pick
SPF 30 or higher, labelled broad spectrum
Look for
No white cast, or a tinted or gel formula
When
Every morning, on your face, neck and ears
Top up
Every couple of hours if you are outside

Protect your glow. Your skin will thank you in ten years.
