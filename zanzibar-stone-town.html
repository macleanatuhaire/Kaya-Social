Travel · Zanzibar

Stone Town

Narrow lanes, painted doors and a long, layered history. Best explored on foot.

Stone Town is the old heart of Zanzibar, and the doors are the first thing you notice.

Heavy carved frames, brass studs and peeling walls line lanes too narrow for cars. Stalls lean against the stone selling painted door panels, woven bags and hats. Turn a corner and there is a stall of spices, or a white house with a balcony, or a fort wall older than anything around it. There is no need for a plan. Walk, and look up.

“No plan needed. Walk, and look up.”

Stone Town also holds hard history. The site of the old slave market sits beside the cathedral, with an exhibit that tells that story plainly. Give it time.
