Always in the bag

Five small things that quietly save the day.

The big things get all the attention. These are the small ones that do the work.

1

Lip therapy

Small enough to forget, until dry air and a long day remind you.

2

Hand lotion

Air conditioning, boda rides and dust are not kind to hands.

3

Wet wipes

For sticky fingers, dusty shoes and the rolex you ate on the way.

4

Gum

Fresh breath for the meeting that appeared out of nowhere.

5

Hair tie

For lifting your braids off your neck on a hot afternoon.

“Small things, big relief.”

What is the one thing you never leave home without?
