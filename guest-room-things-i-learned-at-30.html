Things I learned at 30

Three lessons and one big feeling, from the editor.

1

Say no.

I used to say yes to be liked. Yes to the plans I dreaded, the favours I resented, the invitations that cost me my weekend. At thirty I started saying no, and nobody fell apart. A clear no is kinder than a tired yes, and it leaves room for what I actually want.

2

Get comfortable being uncomfortable.

The things I am proudest of all started with a knot in my stomach. Sending the message I was nervous to send. Trying something new before I felt ready. Starting this website. Comfortable is where I rest. Uncomfortable is where I grow. Now when I feel the knot, I take it as a sign I am close to something.

3

Life is just getting started.

I thought thirty would feel like a finish line. It feels like a start line. I know myself better, I waste less time, and I have more nerve. The best parts are still ahead.

“Life is just getting started.”
