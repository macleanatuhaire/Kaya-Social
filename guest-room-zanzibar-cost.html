What 4 days in Zanzibar really costs

Flights, a bed, food, tours and the $25 you should not skip.

Zanzibar sounds expensive. It does not have to be.

Here is what four days and three nights costs for one person flying from Kampala and staying simply in Stone Town. Every figure is an estimate from current listings, shown in dollars and in shillings at UGX 4,100 to the dollar.

How to spend less

1Share a room. The bed is your biggest choice after the flight, and splitting it saves about UGX 340,000 each.
2Fly out on a Tuesday and back on a Saturday. Those days tend to be the cheapest, and booking about seven weeks ahead helps.
3Avoid January. It is the peak month for flights, and everything costs more.

Before you go

Ugandan passport holders do not need a visa for Tanzania, which includes Zanzibar. Carry your passport, not your national ID, and bring your yellow fever card. Rules change, so check before you fly.
