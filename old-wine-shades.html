Old Wine Shades

A wood-and-glass wine bar above Ntinda.

Warm wood, a very long bar, and treetops through the glass.

Old Wine Shades is a wine bar in Ntinda, set on an upper floor so the trees and rooftops are part of the room. Everything is timber: the beams, the bar front, the tables, and a ceiling cut in a pattern that lets the light through.

The back wall is bottles from end to end, with wine by the glass on the menu and a glass case of cognac and whisky by the window. There is even a blue guitar standing guard in the cabinet.

Seats are low and soft in green and rust, with small round tables, yellow place mats and a plant on each one. The glass walls open the room to the garden, so it feels more like a terrace than a bar.

“Pull up a low chair, and stay for another glass.”
