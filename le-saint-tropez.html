Le Saint Tropez

Hotel, beach club, French restaurant.

Sand-coloured floors, white walls and the sea at the far end of every room.

Le Saint Tropez calls itself a hotel, a beach club and a French restaurant, and the day moves through all three. Lunch sits at long linen tables with woven lamps overhead.

A wall of roses rises behind a cream sofa, a thatched bar looks onto the terrace, and when the light drops, the infinity pool turns gold.

“Lunch by the sea, and stay for the sunset.”
