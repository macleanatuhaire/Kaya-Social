Food · Nungwi, Zanzibar

Sexy Fish

A restaurant on stilts over the beach. Come before sunset.

Sexy Fish stands on stilts over the sand at Nungwi, so the sea is never more than a few steps away.

Dhows rest in the bay and the sky turns gold. Inside, slatted timber, woven lamps and a pair of black giraffes set the mood. Seafood is the order. We had the prawns.

“Arrive for the sunset, stay for dinner.”
