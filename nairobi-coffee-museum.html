Connect Coffee Museum

A walk through the history of coffee, with a good cup at the end.

Connect Coffee Roasters has turned part of its Nairobi space into a museum of coffee.

Illustrated panels follow the bean across the world and into Kenya. Glass cases hold old brewers, grinders and fine china. Then you sit down in a hall of concrete, timber and soft white chairs, with a pastry and a heart in the foam.

“Read the history, then order the coffee.”
