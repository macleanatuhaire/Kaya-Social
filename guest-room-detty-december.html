Detty December is coming

Kampala, we need to talk about your calendar, your wallet and your liver.

December has one job, and it is to make you broke, sleepy and very, very happy.

Every weekend has a concert. Every concert has an after party. Every after party has a second after party, and somebody is already saying "just one more stop". Your calendar is full. Your outfits are planned. Your bank balance is praying.

So here is a small, friendly guide to the season, and a few rules to get you to January in one piece.

A few dates

Sat 28 NovemberB’raka Land, Joshua BarakaThe official kick off. Lugogo Hockey Grounds. Think of it as the starting gun for Detty December, with better music.
Sun 6 DecemberBandali DayOne street, one city, one night. Bandali Rise from midday, and a good reason to keep Sunday free.
Sat 12 DecemberVibe Republic, ft. KonshensJamaican dancehall star Konshens at Lugogo Cricket Oval. Wear shoes you can dance in.
Sat 19 DecemberSpinny & Friends FestivalA full day at Kololo Airstrip with DJ Spinny, AKIO and friends. Pack sunscreen and a water bottle.

Dates, venues and line-ups can change, so check with the organisers before you go.

The rules

1Hydrate. Please.One water for every drink. Water is not a personality flaw, and nobody has ever looked cool while fainting by the speakers.
2Rest a little.Sleep is free, and it is the only thing in December that does not cost a cover charge. Try it between events. Yes, even on a Sunday.
3Eat before you go.Dancing on an empty stomach is how people end up crying over a plate of chips at 3am.
4Plan your ride home.Boda prices go up when the moon comes out. Know how you are getting back before you arrive.
5Pace yourself.December has 31 days. You are in a marathon, not a sprint, and the marathon has a dress code.

“Hydrate. Rest. Repeat.”
