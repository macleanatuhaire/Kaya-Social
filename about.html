About

A quiet place to find good places

Kaya means home in Swahili. Kaya Social is a small guide to the cafes, tables, stays and finds across Uganda and East Africa that are worth your time.

A note from the editor

I made Kaya Social to be a calm corner of the internet for quiet discoveries: the places, the style, and the people worth knowing.

No sponsored rankings, no endless feeds. Just a curated edit from across East Africa, each place shared with a single photograph, a few honest words, and a little context. Enough to spark an idea or point you somewhere worthwhile, so you can close the tab and head out the door.

Then there is The Guest Room, a little space for other people’s thoughts, stories, recommendations and the small, funny things worth sharing. Pull up a chair.

Home is Kampala, so Uganda comes first. From here, I wander to Kenya, Rwanda, Zanzibar and beyond. If there is a space, a creative, or a piece of work you love that belongs here, write to me. Kaya Social is built by the people who shape it.

Maclean Atuhaire

Editor · Kampala
