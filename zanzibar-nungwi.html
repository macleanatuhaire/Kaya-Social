Travel · Zanzibar

Notes from the coast

White sand, warm water and a tide that sets the day. Slow days in Nungwi, at the northern tip of Zanzibar.

We came for the water and stayed for the pace. Nobody on the coast is in a hurry, and after a day, neither were we.

Nungwi sits at the northern tip of Zanzibar, where the beaches are wide, white and soft underfoot. The tide goes out a long way and the whole shoreline changes with it. Dhows sail past, the flats stretch out at low tide, and the sea moves from pale green to deep turquoise in a few steps.

We stayed in an Airbnb, which suited the mood: no lobby, no schedule, just a terrace, a pool and the sound of the water. Mornings on the terrace. Afternoons by the pool. Evenings walking the shoreline until the light ran out.

“The tide decides the schedule.”
