Explore Uganda · Kisoro · 22 November 2026

Virunga Gorilla Marathon

Runners took to the red roads of Kisoro on Saturday, with the volcanoes behind them.

The road out of Kisoro, 21 November 2026.

On Saturday, Mayor's Gardens in Kisoro hosted the Tusker Lite Virunga Gorilla Marathon.

Runners wore green and chose their distance. The bibs on the day showed three: 21km, 10km and 6km. The route climbed through farmland and eucalyptus, over red earth, with a cloud sitting on the volcano ahead.

On the road

Muddy tracks, wet grass and tea-green hills. Some ran alone, some ran in packs, and some walked and took in the view. Kisoro sits in the far south west of Uganda, and the volcanoes of the Virunga range frame almost every turn.

Every finisher earned the view.

Medals out at the finish.

At the finish

Medals around necks, cold drinks on a barrel table and a lot of smiling. Friends posed in matching shirts, and the podium filled up with finishers waving to the camera.
