Oli wa? Broke but still going out

A gentle guide for nights out when the month is long.

Your phone lights up. “Oli wa?”

It is the text that comes the moment a plan is made. You would love to be there, and your budget would love a quieter week. Both can be true. Here are a few small ways to go out and still feel okay in the morning.

1“I’m on my way.”Have a little something to eat first. Eating out adds up quickly, and a full stomach means you can enjoy yourself without ordering because you are hungry.
2“Almost there.”Choose an amount you are happy to spend and bring only that. It is one less thing to think about once the music starts.
3“Which table?”Go a little early. Entry is often kinder on the pocket, the seats are better, and you can leave before it gets busy.
4“Who is paying?”Share the table, the bottle and the ride, and say so at the start. It keeps everyone relaxed, and nobody gets a surprise at the end.
5“Who is dropping who?”Decide how you are getting home before you arrive. It is much easier to plan while it is still bright and calm.
6“One more round?”It is okay to say “I am heading home”. Nobody keeps count of who left first, and your morning self will thank you.

“Broke is only a season. A good night with good people stays with you.”
